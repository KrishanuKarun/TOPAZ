# What it is:
This was a side project I did in the November of 2025 under supervision of Sreekuttan LS, CEO at Bloq Quantum. The objective was to test my innovation and thought process of working.

I was tasked to solve a Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization problem with Quantum Imaginary Time Evolution as the algorithm/ framework. The system size was 4 qubits for ease of calculations, seeing that the objective wasn't to fix a large system size problem, but more of one for proof of concept.
